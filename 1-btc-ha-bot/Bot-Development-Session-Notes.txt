**Strategy logic (identical to BTC — 7 filters must all pass):**
1. M5 ATR above minimum (market is moving)
2. H1 trend clear — 2 consecutive HA candles same direction
3. M1 EMA100 trend agrees
4. H1 and EMA must point same direction (no conflict)
5. Previous M1 candle is a doji (body/range < 15%)
6. M5 HA confirms direction
7. Current M1 HA candle is strong with no opposing wick

**Trade management (auto bot):**
- Break-even: SL moves to entry + spread once 1× ATR profit reached
- Trailing stop: trails at 3× ATR as price moves in favour
- Reversal exit: closes after 2 consecutive opposite M5 HA candles
- Session filter: 07:00–20:00 UTC only
- Daily loss limit: -$150 (stops new entries, not management of open trades)
- Cooldown: 60 seconds between trades
- Reconnection: retries MT5 up to 5 times on disconnect

### 2. ETH Manual Trade Manager (`eth_ha_bot_v1_manual.zip`)
Separate entry point (`main_manual.py`) — you open trades manually in MT5,
the bot detects them and applies all management logic automatically.

**What it manages:**
- Sets SL/TP from ATR if your manual trade has none
- Break-even at 1× ATR profit
- Trailing stop at 3× ATR
- Reversal exit on 2 consecutive M5 HA candles
- Logs every closed trade to `eth_trade_log.csv`

**What it does NOT do in manual mode:**
- Does not open new trades
- Does not block entries outside session hours (you made the call)
- Does not enforce daily loss limit on existing positions

**How to run:**
```
# Auto bot (finds signals and manages)
python main.py

# Manual manager (you enter, bot manages)
python main_manual.py
```

### Risk Management Upgrade (planned, not built yet)
Current system uses fixed $ daily loss limits. Agreed to move to **percentage-based risk**:

| Level | Limit | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Per trade risk | 1–2% of account balance | Dynamic lot sizing |
| Per asset daily loss | 3% of account equity | Stops one bad asset |
| Portfolio daily loss | 6–8% of account equity | Hard stop all assets |

**Dynamic lot sizing formula:**
```
Lot size = (Account balance × Risk %) / (ATR × ATR_MULTIPLIER)
```
Example: $500 account, 1% risk, ATR=8, multiplier=2
→ Lot = $5 / $16 = 0.3125 ETH

**Correlation rule (to add):**
