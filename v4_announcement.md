## What's New in V4

- **Epoch-Aware CNOVA Rewards** — CNOVA minting now follows the full 8-epoch halving schedule (50→40→32→25→20→15→10→5). Re-entry rewards also scale with the current epoch, keeping tokenomics consistent across the entire lifecycle of the system.

- **Re-entry Fee** — A small 5% re-entry fee is applied on each rotation. 70% goes to the Treasury (strengthening the floor price), 20% rewards your original referrer, and 10% goes to operations.

- **Community Wallet — Fully Live** — The Community Wallet is now properly wired end-to-end. Every registration deposits 10% into the pool. The first 10 members earn Tranche A status, the next 10 earn Tranche B. Payouts happen every 25 days via epoch advance.

- **Early Exit Penalty** — CNOVA redeemed within 120 days of joining incurs a sliding penalty (45% → 30% → 15% → 5% → 0%). This protects the floor price and rewards patient holders.

- **Staged Whale Gate** — The fast-track upgrade path to higher tiers now unlocks in stages, giving the community a fair path to grow organically before larger players can skip the queue.

- **L7 → Treasury** — Level 7 chain pay now routes to the Treasury reserve instead of the dev wallet, accelerating floor price growth as the matrix fills.
