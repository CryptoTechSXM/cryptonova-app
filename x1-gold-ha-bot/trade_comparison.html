💡 Bottom line:
Trail management is identical for both paths. The only real gap is at entry — signal trades have a precise, ATR-calibrated SL and risk-sized lot. Adopted trades inherit whatever the manual trader set. If you want full parity, the fix is to normalize the SL on adoption (recalculate to ATR × 1.5 and modify the MT5 order). This is not yet in the bot.
