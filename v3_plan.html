🔒 Sequential Upgrade Path

CNOVA Mining Epochs
8 epochs — reward halves as the network grows. Max supply: 100,000,000 CNOVA
Every time a new member joins Tier 1, they receive CNOVA tokens as a participation reward. The reward halves every 1,000 members (or 30 days, whichever comes first). Early members earn the most — this is Proof of Participation, not mining hardware. Re-entry CNOVA rewards also scale with the current epoch, so the system stays fair throughout all 8 epochs.

⏱ Epoch Triggers
An epoch advances when either condition is met first:

✅ 1,000 new unique Tier 1 members join since the last epoch
✅ 30 days have elapsed since the last epoch advance

This means the system can progress even during slower growth periods.
📊 Re-entry CNOVA — Epoch Scaled
When you complete a cycle and re-enter the queue, you earn a CNOVA bonus based on your tier and the current epoch. The formula is:

Base = max(1 CNOVA per $1 fee, Tier × 2)
Final = Base × (current epoch reward ÷ 50)

Example: Tier 1 re-entry in Epoch 1 = 2 CNOVA. In Epoch 4 = 1 CNOVA.

Early Exit Penalty
Protecting the floor price — rewards patient holders
When you redeem CNOVA for USDC at the floor price within 120 days of joining, a sliding penalty applies. This prevents farm-and-dump behaviour and strengthens the treasury reserve for all holders. Hold longer = full floor price with zero penalty.
Days Since Joining Penalty You Receive To Treasury (80%) To Community (20%)
0 – 30 days 45% 55% of floor value 36% of floor value 9% of floor value
31 – 60 days 30% 70% of floor value 24% of floor value 6% of floor value
61 – 90 days 15% 85% of floor value 12% of floor value 3% of floor value
91 – 120 days 5% 95% of floor value 4% of floor value 1% of floor value
121+ days 0% ✅ 100% of floor value — —
💡 Why This Matters
Every early exit penalty goes back into the system — 80% strengthens the treasury reserve (raising the floor for everyone) and 20% rewards the founding community. Patient holders benefit every time someone exits early.
📅 Clock Starts at Tier 1 Join
The penalty clock is based on when you first registered in Tier 1, not when you acquired CNOVA. This means even CNOVA earned from tier upgrades or re-entries is subject to the same schedule — keeping everything consistent.
