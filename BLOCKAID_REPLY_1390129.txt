Thank you for coming back to us, and for explaining what would help. Attached is a
technical overview of the deployment, written for this request. Three things in it

1. A correction to our own report.

Our submission said "the site requests one token approval: USDC spending for
TierRouter... that is the only token permission the site ever asks for." That was
written in good faith and it is not accurate. The site can also request approvals to
our CouponRegistry (sponsoring another member's entry), our Treasury (token
redemption), and our matrix contracts (a member repaying an advance). We would rather
correct that ourselves than have you find it.

What we should have written is the point that actually matters, and it holds for
every one of them: the site never requests an unlimited allowance. There is no
MaxUint256 or equivalent anywhere in the site source. Every approval is for an exact
amount computed for the action the member just chose, and every spender is one of our
own contracts listed below. That is verifiable in the page source served from the
domain.

2. This is a testnet deployment, and the token is a mock.

Everything runs on Base Sepolia (chain id 84532). The settlement token is our own
MockUSDC at 0x2D8B7b5eDec96bE441b6fb0D45D74a2BcE2C639a — a test token with no market
value, not Circle USDC. There is no mainnet deployment of this system, and no mainnet
asset is reachable from any contract involved.

The contracts were deployed on 2026-08-26 and verified on 2026-08-27. For about a day,

On documentation: we have not had a third-party security audit and we are not going to
imply that we have. What we can send is our own internal work, described as exactly
that. The most useful is PARITY_AUDIT.md, attached: every member-facing claim on the
site listed against the contract function or constant that backs it, under a project
rule that a claim without a named source does not ship — including the rows where we
found the site wrong and corrected it. PIF_CONCEPT.md, also attached, describes the
sponsorship feature behind CouponRegistry.

What CryptoNova is, plainly: a matrix and referral community platform on testnet. A
member pays a $10 test-USDC entry fee, is placed in a referral structure, and earns
test USDC from it. We would rather describe it in those words than in softer ones.

Some context on where this comes from. We have been building CryptoNova for the
better part of three years, and for the past few months it has been on Base
Sepolia in open testing with our community. Members register, report defects through a
bug-report form on the site, and are credited and paid bounties for what they find;
that feedback has shaped the build, including a number of the corrections recorded in
the attached PARITY_AUDIT.md. The testnet phase exists precisely so that the people who
will use this find its faults first.

Parameter changes are governed on chain rather than by us alone. V8Governance
(0x78DC530Ab3D63FC300e14381e27B6eACeD090951, verified) lets any CNOVA holder open a
proposal and vote on it, with voting weight equal to their CNOVA balance, and members
accrue CNOVA by participating. In the interest of being exact rather than flattering:
the governance and direct-sale pages are still behind a "Coming Soon" gate on the
public domains while we complete the testnet phase, so that surface is deployed and
verified but not yet open to members.

The flag currently appears on www.crypto-nova.app, crypto-nova.app, v8.crypto-nova.app,
