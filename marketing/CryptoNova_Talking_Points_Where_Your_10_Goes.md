# CryptoNova — Talking Points: "Where Does Your $10 Go?"

A presenter's script for member calls and community Q&A. Every figure is verified against the
deployed V8.43 contract (not marketing copy). Lead with the honest core, then the breakdown.
Swap `$10 / T1` for the tier you're discussing — the percentages are identical at every tier.

---

## Lead with the truth (say this first, every time)

> "Where do the rewards come from? Other members — and that's the honest answer. This only pays
> as the network grows. No new members, no new rewards. So everything I'm about to show you
> depends on real growth, and I'd rather you know that up front than find out later."

This one line is what keeps the whole pitch credible. Never skip it.

## The breakdown — a $10 Tier 1 entry

Split instantly by the smart contract, the same for every member, no one touching it:

| Slice | Amount | What it is |
|-------|--------|-----------|
| **Your crossing reserve** | **$5.00 (50%)** | Set aside *for you* — pre-funds half of your next re-entry when your matrix fills. |
| **Your instant earn** | **$0.25 (2.5%)** | Credited to your withdrawable balance the moment you join. |
| **Your CNOVA tokens** | **~$0.50 (5% treasury)** | Minted to you on entry, redeemable at a floor price that only rises. |
| **Community — referral + chain pay** | **$1.85 (18.5%)** | $0.50 to your sponsor, $0.27 to each of 5 upline positions. |
| **Community — equalization pool** | **$1.80 (18%)** | Shared across everyone seated in your matrix, each rotation, weighted by depth. |
| **Runs the protocol** | **$0.60 (6%)** | Rescue fund, dev/ops, liquidity, buyback — keeps the machine alive. |

**The headline:** **$5.75 (57.5%) comes back to you** — reserve + instant earn + CNOVA.
**$3.65 (36.5%) is shared with the community.** **$0.60 (6%) runs the protocol.**

> "More than half of what you put in comes back to you — a reserve for your next cycle, instant
> cash-back, and tokens. That's almost never seen in this space. A little over a third is shared
> with the community that makes it work, and a small slice keeps the system running. No one is
> left behind — including you."

## Say it this way (precise phrasing that avoids over-promising)

| Instead of… | Say… | Why |
|-------------|------|-----|
| "$5 cash back to you" | "$5 **set aside for you** to fund your next cycle" | The reserve is yours but **held for re-entry**, not withdrawable — prevents "where's my $5?" confusion. |
| "$0.50 instant CNOVA cash" | "$0.50 in CNOVA tokens, **redeemable at a floor that only goes up**" | CNOVA is minted instantly but vests ~180 days; its value is at the floor, and can grow. |
| "$4.25 goes to uplines" | "$3.65 **shared with the community**, $0.60 **runs the protocol**" | More accurate *and* more impressive — shows money isn't lost to overhead. |
| "guaranteed" / "returns" | "depends on network growth — nothing is guaranteed" | Keeps the pitch honest and defensible. |

## The three questions members always ask

**"So the money comes from other people?"**
> Yes — like every network, it pays as it grows. The difference here is that no company controls
> it, more than half of every entry comes back to the person who paid it, and every rule is on-chain
> where you can verify it. It's not a promise of profit; it's a transparent system that rewards growth.

**"Is my $5 reserve gone?"**
> No — it's held in your name to pre-fund your next crossing, so you don't pay full price to continue.
> If your earnings plus the reserve don't cover a crossing, you're parked safely and can self-rescue
> for just the shortfall — no debt, nothing lost.

**"What if the network stops growing?"**
> Then cycles slow down — honestly. That's why we only ever say: participate with what you can
> afford, treat it as high-risk, and verify everything on-chain before you join. We'd rather keep you
> than oversell you.

## Compliance guardrails (for whoever is presenting)

- Never say "guaranteed," "returns," "investment," or promise a dollar figure or timeline.
- Always pair the upside with "depends on growth / high-risk / only what you can afford to lose."
- The reserve is "held for you," not "cash you can withdraw."
- CNOVA is a utility token with a mechanical floor — never a "stablecoin" or a "sure thing."
- All numbers verified against V8.43 contract source as of 2026-07-25. If the contract changes,
  re-verify before presenting.
